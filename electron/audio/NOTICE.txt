Audio and video components are downloaded unmodified and executed as separate programs.
FFmpeg and ffprobe 8.1.2: https://ffmpeg.org/
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz
Binary distributor, exact dependency versions and build/source instructions:
https://github.com/shaka-project/static-ffmpeg-binaries/releases/tag/n8.1.2-1
https://github.com/shaka-project/static-ffmpeg-binaries/tree/n8.1.2-1
These builds use GPLv3-or-later (see COPYING.GPLv3). The license reported by
each executable is saved as ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt or ffprobe-LICENSE.txt.

Audio format adapters are adapted from Unlock Music:
https://git.unlock-music.dev/um/web
https://github.com/Magiicccc/music-batch-unlock/tree/main/src/decrypt
Static QMC reference: commit 6e8e8e87942da389fa6a4d04f10d4cb5de06849c, src/decrypt/qmc_cipher.ts.
CrewFlow uses bounded, streaming Node.js adapters for NCM, UC, KWM and static QMC only.
